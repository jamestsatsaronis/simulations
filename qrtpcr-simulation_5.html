Each cycle, template dsDNA approximately doubles (efficiency-dependent). At 95% efficiency, copies increase by a factor of 1.95× per cycle. SYBR Green I intercalates into dsDNA, fluorescing ~1000× brighter than when free in solution, so fluorescence is proportional to total dsDNA. The bar chart shows the exponential accumulation on a log10 scale; notice each bar grows by a near-constant increment (log10 1.95 ≈ 0.29) until the plateau phase.

PCR efficiency is calculated from the slope: E = 10(−1/slope) − 1. An ideal efficiency of 100% yields a slope of −3.32, meaning each 10-fold dilution shifts the Ct by ~3.32 cycles.
