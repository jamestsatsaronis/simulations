How the techniques behave
  • G-50 gel filtration separates by size: proteins above ~30 kDa run fast (void), smaller ones are retarded. It cannot separate two proteins of the same size.
  • DEAE (pH 7.5) is an anion exchanger: it binds proteins that are negatively charged at pH 7.5 (pI < 7.5); positive/neutral proteins flow through. Bound proteins are recovered with a high-salt wash.
  • SDS-PAGE shows subunit mass — one band per polypeptide, so the number of bands tells you whether a tube is pure. Native gel (pH 8.8) shows charge, and colour / UV-Vis distinguish the haem/iron proteins.
