Biochemistry simulations

Interactive teaching tools for biochemistry and molecular biology students. Each one runs in your browser with nothing to install.

Amino acids and proteins

  • Amino acid code drillMatch amino acid names with their three-letter and one-letter codes.
  • Side chain classificationClassify amino acid side chains, for example polar, charged or hydrophobic.
  • Charge at pHPractise working out the net charge of amino acids and peptides.
  • Ramachandran explorerExplore phi/psi backbone angles with linked 3D views.
  • Secondary structure viewsInteractive 3D views of protein secondary structure.

Purification and chromatography

  • Ion exchange chromatographyAn interactive chromatography activity.
  • Protein purification tutorialA guided tutorial activity on protein purification.
  • Protein purification labA virtual lab with steps interleaved with visual gels.

Enzymes and molecular techniques

  • Enzyme kineticsExplore Michaelis-Menten kinetics.
  • Reaction coordinate builderBuild reaction coordinate (energy profile) diagrams.
  • qRT-PCR simulationRun a SYBR Green qRT-PCR assay.
